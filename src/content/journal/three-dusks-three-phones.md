The oldest photograph on this site is
[*Twilight Bridge Over Still Waters*](/photo/twilight-bridge-over-still-waters). A
cable-stayed bridge in silhouette across flat water, pink and blue cloud above it, and the
stubs of old pier pilings standing in the foreground. The capture time inside the file is
17:34 on 23 February 2020. OnePlus 7 Pro, 27mm equivalent, f/1.7, 1/50s at ISO 250.

Nearly four years later, on New Year's Day 2024,
[*Twilight Over the Back Bay*](/photo/twilight-over-the-back-bay) looks down on a city from
above at dusk: a dark glass tower in the middle, low brick streets
running out to the water, and the same pink and blue across the sky. Galaxy S22 Ultra, 23mm,
f/1.8, 1/180s at ISO 200.

And in April, [*Portals to Twilight*](/photo/portals-to-twilight): a weathered boardwalk
running straight away from the camera through a line of timber arches, each one smaller
than the last, under a sky banded pink and blue. Galaxy S24 Ultra, 23mm, f/1.7, 1/120s at
ISO 200.

Three phones, all on their main camera, all within a few millimetres of the same focal
length, all at fixed apertures of f/1.7 or f/1.8, and all at ISO 200 or 250. The exposures
sit within about two and a third stops of each other, the 2020 bridge being the darkest. In
these three, at least, dusk is about as repeatable as anything a phone gets pointed at. The
light changes by the minute, but the window when that colour is in the sky is narrow enough
that each attempt lands in roughly the same place.

Portals to Twilight has one oddity. Its file name is stamped 21:54. The capture time
recorded inside the file is 20:03, almost two hours earlier. The sky in the frame is still
coloured, which fits eight in the evening in April far better than ten, and the capture time
is the one the camera writes at the moment the shutter fires, so that is the one I trust.
Where the file name's time came from, I do not know. Even the file's own record of when it
was last modified says 20:03.

It is a small thing, but it is the kind of thing this site keeps running into. The records
are good, and occasionally two of them contradict each other, and the only way to tell
which is right is to look at the photograph and ask which one it agrees with.
