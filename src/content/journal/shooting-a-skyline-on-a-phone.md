## What the frames actually record

**Six frames on 31 August 2024**, every one on a **Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra**. No Canon
that day.

The phone's whole lens range got used: *Neon Pulse of the Metropolis* at **23mm
equivalent** on the wide camera, *Beacon of the Skyline* at **115mm equivalent** on the
super-telephoto. Four architecture frames, two night.

The exposure spread is the interesting part — **1/2800s** on *Monolith and Ribs* down to
**1/60s** on *Concrete Canopy Under Stormy Skies* and *Neon Pulse*. ISO stays between
**32 and 100**, so the light was good even at the end.

*Concrete Canopy Under Stormy Skies* says weather turned.
