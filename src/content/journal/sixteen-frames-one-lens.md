Sixteen photographs in this archive came from a folding phone, eight from a Galaxy Z Fold6
and eight from a Z Fold7. Both phones carry three cameras on the back: an ultrawide, a main
wide camera, and a 3x telephoto. Every one of the sixteen came off the main camera.

Fourteen are at its native 23mm equivalent. Two report 36mm and 44mm, but the lens field
still names the wide camera, so those are crops from the same sensor rather than a different
lens. The ultrawide and the telephoto do not appear once.

That is odd next to the S24 Ultra. Its frames here use all four of its lenses, and the one
that did the most work was the long one: ten of its twenty-five frames are at 115mm.

Half of the sixteen are twenty-five-second exposures of the night sky, most of them written
up in [Three Nights, One Exposure](/journal/three-nights-one-exposure). The other eight are
everything else, and they are all over the place in a way the night frames are not.

[*Harbor Remnants*](/photo/harbor-remnants) is May 2025 on the Fold6: a sun-dried fish tail
hanging from a rope-wrapped dock piling, deep blue water and a clear sky behind it. f/1.8,
1/1700s, ISO 25. [*Grounded Speed and Airborne
Dreams*](/photo/grounded-speed-and-airborne-dreams) is the next month, a yellow sports car
parked in an empty lot at sunset with a passenger jet coming in above it, at 1/2000s and ISO
40. Both are the phone with far more light than it needs and nothing to do about it but
shorten the shutter.

[*Stitches in Time*](/photo/stitches-in-time) is December 2025 on the Fold7, in black and
white: a shop interior with rows of old sewing machines on open shelving, a wool coat on a
rail and a leather armchair below. 1/120s at ISO 125.
[*Neon Alleyway*](/photo/neon-alleyway) is May 2026, a covered brick passage with every wall,
beam and roof panel covered in dense graffiti, opening onto a lit street at the far end. 1/20s
at ISO 1250. From sunlit water to an alley at night, on a main camera fixed at f/1.8 on
one phone and f/1.7 on the other, the only things that moved were the shutter and the ISO.

Across the sixteen, the shutter runs from 1/2000s to twenty-five seconds, which is more than
fifteen stops, with the aperture fixed on each phone and, two crops aside, one focal length.
ISO runs from 25 to 3200.

The files do not say why the other two cameras never appear. The telephoto on the Fold is a
3x rather than the 5x on the S24, so it reaches less far, but it is there, and in this
archive it was never used. What the sixteen frames do show is that one camera, used for
about a year across two phones, was enough to cover a harbour in full sun, a shop, an alley
and the Milky Way.
