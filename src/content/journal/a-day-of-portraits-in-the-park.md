## What the frames actually record

This was the biggest single shoot in the archive: **15 frames on 23 August 2026**, all on
the **Canon EOS R5 Mark II**, split across two lenses.

The **RF 35mm f/1.8 Macro** did the close work — *A Sunny Day in the Park*, *Strokes of
Identity*, *A Quiet Encounter in the Green*. The **RF 70-200mm f/2.8 L** did everything at
distance: *Sunflowers in the Summer Shade* and *Reflections by the Waterside* are both at
the long end, wide open.

Shutter speeds run from **1/5000s down to 1/30s**, and ISO from **100 to 400** — so this
started in hard daylight and ran into the evening. *Crimson Horizon* is the one night frame
from the same day, at 1/30s and ISO 400.
