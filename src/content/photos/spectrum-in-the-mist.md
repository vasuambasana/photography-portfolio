I caught this right as the afternoon sun hit the mist at just the right angle, creating a vivid prism at the base of the falls. The stark face of the granite cliff softened for a few brief minutes as the light shifted across the valley. Framing the shot through the pine branches helped anchor the immense scale of the landscape.
