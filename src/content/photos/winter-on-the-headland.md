I scrambled down the freezing granite rocks just as the sun dipped beneath the cloud deck, casting a warm glow across the icy coast. The sharp contrast between the cold air, snow-dusted shoreline, and brief golden light made the wait worthwhile. It was a quiet winter evening where everything seemed to hold still for a few minutes.
