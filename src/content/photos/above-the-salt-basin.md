I waited at the ridge as the sun dipped behind the western mountains, casting a final streak of warmth across the valley floor. The contrast between the cool, heavy twilight sky and the bright reflective channels below created a rare moment of balance in the landscape. I kept the frame wide to capture the sheer scale and quiet stillness of the desert at dusk.
