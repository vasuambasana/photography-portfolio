Against a vibrant sunset of deep blues and magenta clouds, the iconic Leonard P. Zakim Bunker Hill Bridge stands tall alongside an industrial truss bridge. The dark silhouettes of the bridges and a weathered wooden pier cast long, rhythmic reflections across the calm, glassy surface of the harbor.
