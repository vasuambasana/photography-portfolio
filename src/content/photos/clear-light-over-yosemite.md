I stopped at the overlook just as the morning sun fully lit the valley floor, bringing out the sharp contrast between the granite walls and the deep pine forest below. The sheer scale of this landmark is always humbling, but on this day, the exceptional clarity of the air drew me in. I wanted to capture a clean, quiet perspective of the valley in its truest element.
