I stayed at the overlook long after sunset, watching the deep blue mountain ridges fade into shadow. Just before darkness settled completely, a narrow rift in the clouds caught one last brilliant line of light along the horizon. It was a brief, quiet moment that made the cold wait entirely worth it.
