I stopped here because of the contrast between the building's sharp industrial grid and the organic shapes of the bare trees. The late afternoon sunlight caught the lower branches just enough to bring a touch of warmth to the cold glass and steel. I wanted to capture how the structure cuts cleanly into the sky while still sharing space with the natural elements around it.
