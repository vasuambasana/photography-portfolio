I stopped near the ridge top as the soft overcast light settled across the valley, bringing out the distinct blue haze between each peak. The contrast between the sharp evergreen canopy in front of me and the endless fading layers in the distance made the whole landscape feel perfectly still. It was one of those quiet moments on the trail where you just stand and take in the scale of it all.
