I waited down in the narrow canyon until light bounced off the upper rim, casting soft violet shadows against the warm, glowing rock. The contrast between the rigid stone and its fluid, wave-like shapes is what drew me to framing this upward perspective. Standing at the canyon floor, the quiet stillness of the space made the steep sandstone walls feel almost weightless.
