I noticed this small group of sparrows gathering on the patio railing during a quiet, overcast morning. What drew me in was the quick moment of an adult feeding its fledgling right next to the scattered seeds. The muted light gave the whole scene a peaceful tone, letting the warm browns in their feathers step forward against the cool background.
