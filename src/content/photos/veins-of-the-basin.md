Standing high above the valley, I was drawn to the contrast between the warm, lit ridge beneath me and the cool blue shadows covering the basin floor. The quiet morning light rendered the white salt channels like delicate drawn lines across the dark earth. I framed this shot to capture the serpentine curve leading into the isolated salt pool below.
