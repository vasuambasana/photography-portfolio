I stepped outside just as a brief summer shower cleared, when the air was still cool and damp. I was drawn to the contrast between the deep shadows beneath the hostas and the bright light catching fresh water along their ribbed leaves. It was a simple, quiet moment in the garden that felt worth slowing down for.
