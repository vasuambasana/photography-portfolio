I arrived at the overlook just before sunrise while the valley was still steeped in cool twilight shadows. What drew me in was the quiet contrast between the vibrant sky and the ancient mineral layers exposed across the canyon floor. It felt like watching the earth slowly wake up in absolute stillness.
