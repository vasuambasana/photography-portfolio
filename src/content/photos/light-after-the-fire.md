I stopped along this ridge just as the sun dropped behind the haze, casting a warm orange glow across the fire-scarred slope. The stark contrast between the burnt trees, lingering snow, and soft evening light created a stillness I couldn't walk past. It felt like a quiet moment of calm in a landscape taking its time to recover.
