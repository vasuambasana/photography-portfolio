I wanted to capture the striking mineral oxidation in late afternoon, when the low light deepens the turquoise and magenta bands in the rock. Seeing tiny figures move along the ridge line provided an immediate sense of scale against millions of years of geological history. The contrast between the harsh desert terrain and these quiet, painted hues felt almost unreal.
